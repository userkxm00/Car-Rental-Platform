# Reference Audit — Fleet Management Systems

## Sources

- https://github.com/caliphviper/Fleet-management-system
- https://github.com/navodya0/FMS

## Useful patterns

The repositories demonstrate useful fleet-oriented concerns beyond simple rental CRUD:

- Vehicle status and odometer.
- Service/maintenance history.
- Expiry dashboards for insurance and other vehicle documents.
- Inspection reports.
- Post-check verification/approval workflows.
- Rental conflict prevention.
- Role-specific dashboards.
- Procurement/accounting concepts where fleet maintenance is material.
- PDF generation for operational documents.

## Adopt for our product

- Document expiry center.
- Maintenance lifecycle and readiness.
- Inspection review workflow.
- Explicit rental conflict protection.
- Role-aware operational dashboards.
- Auditable operational actions.
- Export/print support for agency documents.

## Improve for a rental business

- Connect maintenance/readiness directly to availability.
- Connect inspections to rental contracts and evidence.
- Add customer-facing issue/report workflows.
- Add profitability and utilization analytics.
- Add mobile-first staff operations.

## Do not copy blindly

These projects are useful references, but their domain assumptions are not sufficient for a SaaS rental marketplace. Their procurement/accounting workflows should only be adopted when supported by our requirements.
