# Marketplace References — Audit Notes

## Aggar-App

Repository: https://github.com/Aggar-rent-a-ride/Aggar-App

Value:
- marketplace with customer/renter/admin roles
- advanced search and location services
- real-time messaging concepts
- push notifications
- vehicle detail and booking flows
- separate role-oriented interfaces

Use in our project:
- study role separation and marketplace journeys
- evaluate chat/notification ideas for future phases

Do not copy:
- implementation, branding, architecture, or UI wholesale.

## Selection principle

These references expand the audit beyond BookCars. BookCars remains the primary architecture reference; regional and marketplace repositories are used to identify useful workflows and gaps.

Every adopted idea must be reconciled with our own business rules, security model, multi-tenancy model, and Algeria/Maghreb requirements.
