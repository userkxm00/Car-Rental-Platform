# 46 — Go-to-Market Strategy

## Objective

Turn the Release 1 product into a measurable Algeria-first pilot, then expand the marketplace network.

## Initial ICP

Primary agency: small-to-mid-size car-rental business with 3–100 vehicles, one or more branches, manual booking workflows, and a need for better fleet/booking/inspection control.

Primary customer: person searching for a rental car by location and dates who values availability, price transparency, agency trust and convenient pickup.

## Agency acquisition sequence

1. Build a list of local agencies by city/wilaya.
2. Prioritize agencies with visible inventory, active social presence and manual operational pain.
3. Offer a low-friction pilot: guided onboarding + configurable trial.
4. Start with 2–3 agencies as design partners.
5. Measure activation, booking volume, operational time saved and retention.
6. Expand to 10 agencies only after the core workflow is stable.

## Sales/onboarding funnel

Prospect → demo → account created → agency setup → first vehicle → first booking → first completed rental → paid conversion/renewal.

Track drop-off at every stage.

## Pricing strategy

Do not hard-code prices into the application architecture. Platform Admin configures plans and terms.

Start with a small number of simple plans and validate willingness to pay with the pilot. Avoid charging for basic data integrity, safety or historical records.

## Marketplace growth

Marketplace acquisition is two-sided:

- supply: recruit reliable agencies and complete their public profiles;
- demand: local SEO, useful location/airport pages, social content, partnerships and referral channels.

Do not spend heavily on ads before there is enough supply and conversion evidence.

## Partnerships

Potential channels:
- hotels/accommodations
- travel agencies
- airport transfer/service ecosystems
- tourism businesses
- corporate travel providers
- local automotive/service partners

Partnerships require explicit attribution and commercial rules before implementation.

## Metrics

Agency:
- activation rate
- time to first vehicle
- time to first booking
- weekly active staff
- completed rentals
- retention
- support load
- plan conversion

Marketplace:
- search volume
- search-to-offer-view
- offer-to-booking
- booking completion
- repeat customer rate
- location/city demand
- zero-result rate

Commercial:
- acquisition cost
- subscription revenue
- marketplace revenue where enabled
- advertising revenue where enabled
- churn
- estimated LTV

These are business metrics, not reasons to compromise availability, ranking fairness or tenant isolation.

## Pilot program

Pilot with 2–3 real agencies before broad launch. Collect weekly structured feedback, prioritize repeated operational problems, and document evidence before changing product scope.

## Rule

Go-to-market decisions may change pricing and activation configuration, but must not silently change the frozen architecture or core rental integrity rules.
