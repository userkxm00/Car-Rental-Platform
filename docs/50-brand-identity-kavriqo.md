# KAVRIQO — Brand Identity Baseline

## Status

**Approved working brand — 2026-08-29**

## Official product name

**Kavriqo**

Primary display form:

`KAVRIQO`

## Product descriptor

**Car Rental & Mobility Platform**

The descriptor explains the category without limiting the brand to a single implementation or market.

## Product architecture naming

- **Kavriqo Marketplace** — customer-facing multi-agency marketplace.
- **Kavriqo for Agencies** — agency SaaS and management suite.
- **Kavriqo Agency App** — Agency Operations Mobile App.
- **Kavriqo Platform** — private Platform Owner Control Plane.

## Brand principles

1. Modern and trustworthy.
2. Regional-first but not region-locked.
3. Useful to both renters and professional agencies.
4. Technological without looking overly corporate.
5. Clear enough for Arabic, French and English interfaces.

## Visual direction

The brand system should support:

- a geometric **K** symbol suitable for favicon and app icon;
- a clean wordmark for web and documents;
- responsive logo variants;
- light and dark backgrounds;
- subtle depth and motion;
- selective 3D treatment for hero/onboarding/brand moments;
- restrained motion in operational screens to preserve speed and clarity.

The agent must not invent unrelated logos, mascots, gradients, or visual identities per screen. The final visual tokens belong in the design-system implementation.

## Product UI rule

Branding must remain separate from business truth. Logo/animation/visual effects must never obscure:

- availability;
- price;
- booking state;
- payment status;
- inspection evidence;
- security actions;
- accessibility.

## App and browser surfaces

## Naming caution

This is a product/brand decision, not a legal trademark clearance. Before commercial registration, domain purchase, or public store submission, perform a current legal/domain/app-store availability check in intended markets.
