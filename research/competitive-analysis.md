# Competitive Feature Synthesis

## Reference set

1. BookCars — primary mature open-source car rental platform.
2. Autorockin — regional Morocco-oriented marketplace reference.
3. Fleet-management-system — fleet health, maintenance, inspection and reporting reference.
4. FMS — inspection, rental conflict, expiry and role workflow reference.
5. Car Rental Booking Pro — advanced concept catalog for GPS, visual damage, multi-vendor, wallet and omnichannel messaging.
6. Ipark — map/search/location interaction reference.
7. Car Rental Agreement — multilingual contract/PDF reference.

## Synthesis

### Core parity features

We should match established expectations:
- Vehicle fleet management.
- Availability/scheduling.
- Booking lifecycle.
- Dynamic/effective-dated pricing.
- Customer accounts/history.
- Payments/deposits/refunds.
- Contracts and receipts.
- Locations/maps.
- Notifications.
- Mobile apps.
- Multilingual UI.

### Our differentiators

- Map-first discovery and location operations.
- Strong Arabic RTL implementation.
- Algeria/Maghreb payment and manual reconciliation model.
- Exception-first owner dashboard.
- Staff mobile operational workflow.
- Inspection/damage evidence lifecycle.
- Vehicle readiness state after return.
- Vehicle profitability/ROI.
- Transparent price breakdown and price history for customer where applicable.
- AI assistance grounded in authorized operational data.
- Provider-neutral map/payment/storage integrations.
- Architecture prepared for agencies, branches and future marketplace mode.

## Feature hierarchy

### Tier A — must be excellent

Booking, availability, pricing, fleet, customers, pickup/return, inspection, payments/billing, contracts, map/locations, Arabic/French/English, RBAC and tenant isolation.

### Tier B — operational advantage

Staff tasks, readiness, attention center, maintenance, vehicle profitability, smart notifications, partner/referral tracking.

### Tier C — advanced differentiation

AI assistant, damage comparison, smart pricing, demand heatmaps, telematics/GPS, loyalty/wallet, advanced marketplace commissions.

## Product rule

A feature is not considered complete when the UI exists. It is complete only when its domain model, authorization, business rules, error handling, audit behavior, automated tests and documentation are coherent.
